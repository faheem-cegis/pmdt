About the PMDT Self-Assessment:

The PMDT self-assessment is a tool that provides a snapshot of personnel management maturity across four key functions - Staffing, Recruitment, Learning & Development, and Performance Management.

The objective is to identify strengths and areas of development to support more effective personnel management practices within your organisation / department.
